## 1. Holder rewards — think it through (design open)

Pump.fun has no native holders-rewards mode (mogz does; POLY/USA/DAYWALKER used it). On pump it takes a **distributor job** on top of the fee sweeper: a set % of each coin's claimed creator fees routes to a rewards pool, distributed pro-rata to holders on a snapshot schedule.

Open questions for the crew:
- Fee split: what % to holders vs bankroll vs the per-coin Dex fund?
- Paid in SOL, or in the coin itself (the Downald DJT-rewards pattern — distributor never built)?
- Snapshot/distribution cadence; minimum holding to qualify (anti-dust, anti-sybil)?
- Does the bot's own dev-buy position count in the snapshot? (muse's proposal: no.)
Why it matters: rewards are the reason to HOLD a mogged coin instead of flipping it, and every distribution is a public event (Telegram + X, automated).

## 2. Upkeep cadence (Sneaks's ruling)

Simple upkeep by Muse once or twice a day; everything else automated. The daily pass: listener alive, gates firing, ledger reconciled, fees claimed, Dex funds tracked, content queue full, Telegram feed posting, bankroll vs caps. Exceptions surface to Sneaks; routine passes stay silent.
