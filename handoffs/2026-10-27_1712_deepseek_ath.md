## 3. Gaps FOMO Has That ATH Could Own

1. **Slippage / hidden execution cost** — FOMO's default slippage was called ~45% and effective fees >2% for active traders vs the 0.5% headline (AppBrain 1-star, Sep 20 2026); a $30k fat-finger buy became ~$2k slippage in seconds (AirdropAlert). *Pain:* users lose on fill, not just fee. *ATH:* exact pre-trade quote on Meteora DBC, user-set slippage cap, signed non-custodially.

2. **Small-trade economics** — $1/min fee makes <$300 accounts unprofitable (BrokerListings); Solana min up to 0.50 USDC under $100. *Pain:* small traders bleed on flat fees. *ATH:* 50 bps curve (3:1:1 split), no flat minimum; sub-$100 Solana trades stay viable.

3. **Withdrawals / support** — withdraw blocked by "verification issue" after easy deposit; a deposit charged but never credited ($100 case); no live chat, slow generic replies (JustUseApp, BrokerListings). *Pain:* funds trapped, no recourse. *ATH:* non-custodial — funds never held, so there is no withdrawal gate to clear.

4. **Swipe-buy price risk** — swipe can't lock price and redirects away from the chart (BrokerListings). *Pain:* blind buys. *ATH:* confirm screen shows locked quote + fee before signing.

5. **Manual-only copy** — alert + tap, never auto-copy (fomo.family). *Pain:* followers miss fills. *ATH:* push on open + pre-filled buy, still user-signed.

6. **Leaderboard segments** — top traders are long-term holders; no day/meme-trader segments (BrokerListings). *Pain:* short-horizon traders have no peers to follow. *ATH:* time-windowed meme/day segments from on-chain wallet history.

7. **Sell lag in volatility** — slow sells / execution lag (BrokerListings). *Pain:* exits miss. *ATH:* DBC gives a deterministic exit at the stated curve price.

## 5. Risks to Copying FOMO

- **Perps / regulatory** — FOMO added perps "NOT for US persons" (Jun 2026). Copying that drags in derivatives/geo-compliance obligations a small team can't staff. ATH is Solana-only spot, no legal infra, PWA skips store review but not securities law. **VERIFIED** (perps-not-US from third-party guides) / **INFERENCE** (ATH exposure). *Mitigate:* stay spot-only; geoblock US if perps ever added; zero leverage claims.
- **Custody / embedded wallet** — FOMO uses Privy Shamir-split key, Apple/Google login, exportable, no seed shown. ATH is non-custodial (users sign, app holds no keys). ATH is safer on custody risk but loses social-login onboarding and can't promise FOMO's recovery UX. **VERIFIED** (Privy/Shamir/login from pack). *Mitigate:* keep non-custodial; keep wallet-adapter; never claim "no seed shown" recovery.
- **Fee / slippage model** — headline 0.50% (0.45% referral) but reviews allege 45% default slippage, $1/min fee, $30k→$2k slippage, slow sells in volatility. ATH's Meteora DBC moves fast on meme coins, so mis-set slippage burns users harder. **VERIFIED** (headline fees) / **INFERENCE** (ATH impact; complaint figures single-source). *Mitigate:* hardcap slippage, live price, no price-lock-free swipe-buy, no minimum fee.
- **Leaderboard gaming / social proof** — public feed + leaderboards incl. "Losers"; top traders are long-term holders, rugs/scams hosted. Solana memes are rug-prone, so an ATH leaderboard invites fake-volume gaming. **VERIFIED** (feed/leaderboards/rugs from reviews). *Mitigate:* on-chain-verifiable PnL, keep "Losers" transparency, refuse hosted rugs.
- **Copy-trading claims vs manual reality** — FOMO is *manual* copy only (alert + tap), not auto-copy. ATH can't build real auto-copy infra; overclaiming destroys trust. **VERIFIED** (manual-only from pack). *Mitigate:* label manual explicitly, never market auto-copy.
- **Multi-chain abstraction** — 6 chains, one USD balance, no bridging is FOMO's core moat. ATH is Solana-only and can't compete on that axis. **VERIFIED** (6-chain claim). *Mitigate:* lean into Solana-native speed/cheapness; don't promise cross-chain.
- **Referral economics** — 25% of referee fees + 10% off. ATH's 50 bps 3:1:1 split leaves thin margin to fund a 25% referral without gutting creator/platform share. **VERIFIED** (terms) / **INFERENCE** (ATH margin). *Mitigate:* don't replicate 25% until unit economics prove; protect the fee split.
