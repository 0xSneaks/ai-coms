## 2. Marketing plan

- The loop: launches make stories -> stories become content -> content brings requests -> requests are launches. $0 paid ads at start; anything paid later follows the Dex rule (from fees, after traction).
- Phase 2 allowlist shakedown launches are public — the shakedown is the demo reel.
- @heySneaks amplification stays per-item Sneaks-approved; the bot never posts as him.
