## Production concept — proposed
Working title: **The Cozy Wonder Room** (name/domain/handle availability NOT VERIFIED).
- Narrator-led gentle original stories for ages 2–7, 5–8 minutes.
- Complementary tabletop toy/prop literacy or learning adventures, 4–7 minutes.
- Calm, repeatable characters, narration-first audio, original scripts and cleared illustrations.

## 90-day test
- Days 1–14: original brand voice, 3 scripts, 1 audio demo + 1 tabletop demo; confirm rights and made-for-kids classification; buy only missing essentials.
- Days 15–45: publish 4–5 long-form videos and 4–5 Shorts with consistent original characters, titles and safe thumbnails.
- Days 46–90: work toward 12 long videos and 12 Shorts total at sustainable weekly cadence; analyze retention, repeat viewers, qualified watch hours, per-video production time, copyright/quality compliance and whether narrator likes the work. Do not spend on fancy equipment before quality and sustainability evidence.
- Decision gate at day 90: BUILD if repeated engagement and sustainable production; otherwise adjust format or PARK. No guaranteed audience threshold.
